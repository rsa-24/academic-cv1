## Overview

This project was completed at Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Educational and Research Institute.

I studied deterministic and probabilistic primality-testing algorithms and their mathematical foundations.

## Algorithms Studied

- Sieve of Eratosthenes
- Lucas–Lehmer Test
- AKS Primality Test
- Solovay–Strassen Test
- Miller–Rabin Test

The project included an analysis of their complexity, advantages, and limitations.

## Cryptographic Relevance

I examined the relevance of efficient primality testing to public-key cryptography, particularly RSA.
